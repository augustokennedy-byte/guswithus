About GUS

Growth. Unity. Success. Three words, one promise to the young people of Queens.

GUS — Growth, Unity & Success, Inc. is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit serving underserved youth and families, ages 8–24, in Jamaica, South Jamaica, South Ozone Park, and the Far Rockaways in Queens, New York. Our community knows us as GUS With Us — and guswithus.org is our official home online.

Our mission: To empower underserved youth and communities by providing transformative programs that promote personal growth, strength, unity, and long-term success.

Our story so far

We were incorporated in New York in 2025 and served roughly 75 young people in our first full year — powered entirely by individual and community donations. We don't operate out of a single building. Our programs meet young people where they already are: in schools and community spaces across Queens, with referrals coming through school counselors and community partners.

What we do

Financial Literacy

Hands-on money and entrepreneurship education inside the worlds kids already love — from Minecraft and Roblox economies to AI and social media.

Clothing Assistance

Clothing collected, professionally cleaned, and set out on racks so families choose what they need with dignity. No application. No means test.

Academic Support

Tutoring, mentorship, and executive-function coaching in small groups, backed by a volunteer corps.

Athletic Development

Basketball, floor hockey, golf, and fitness programming that builds confidence and community.

Vocational Training

Career readiness and certifications for young adults ages 16–24.
