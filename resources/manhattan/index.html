Everything you need, at your fingertips.

Start Here 3 resources

ACCESS NYC — check what you qualify for

Answer a few questions and it shows you every city, state and federal benefit you can get. Takes about 10 minutes. No account, no name, nothing saved.

Also in Spanish, Chinese, Bengali, Russian, Haitian Creole, Korean, Arabic, Urdu, French and Polish.

Start the screener

ACCESS HRA — the application itself

One form applies you for SNAP (food stamps), Cash Assistance and Medicaid at the same time.

Apply now (718) 557-1399 — HRA Infoline

NYC 311

The city's help line for anything you cannot find here. Free, 24 hours, over 175 languages.

Open 311 Call 311

Food 7 resources

Right here in Manhattan

Holy Apostles Soup Kitchen

New York's largest soup kitchen. A hot meal every weekday, holidays included. No questions, no paperwork, just come.

296 Ninth Avenue, New York, NY 10001

Hours and services (212) 924-0167

SNAP (food stamps)

Monthly money on a card for groceries. If you qualify for emergency processing you can get benefits in about 5 days.

Not sure if you qualify? Check first at access.nyc.gov.

Apply now (718) 557-1399

Plentiful — find a pantry and book a time

Shows the food pantries closest to you and lets you reserve a slot so you are not standing in line.

No smartphone? Text the word FOOD to 726879 from any phone.

WIC — food for pregnant women, babies and kids under 5

Free healthy food, formula, breastfeeding support and health referrals. You can qualify even if you work.

Housing & Shelter 9 resources

Homebase — stop an eviction before it happens

Free help with back rent, landlord problems, and one-time emergency money to keep you in your apartment. Go here before you lose the place.

There is a Homebase office in every borough.

Find your office

EvictionFreeNYC — free lawyer for eviction

If you got court papers, you have a right to a free lawyer in every NYC ZIP code. Immigration status does not matter.

Get a lawyer (718) 557-1379

One-Shot Deal — emergency money for rent

A one-time grant for back rent, a security deposit, movers, or a utility shutoff. Apply through ACCESS HRA.

How to apply (718) 557-1399

Enter shelter tonight

Intake is open 24 hours. Men: 8 East 3rd St, Manhattan. Women: 114 Snediker Ave, Brooklyn or 1122 Franklin Ave, Bronx. Adult families: 333 Bowery, Manhattan. Families with children: PATH, 151 East 151st St, Bronx.

These addresses changed in August 2026. Call 311 to confirm before you travel.

Shelter info Call 311 first

Housing Court Answers

Free advice line if you are behind on rent, in Housing Court, or facing a lockout. They explain what the papers mean.

Get answers (212) 962-4795

NYC Housing Connect — affordable apartment lottery

The city's official list of income-restricted apartments. Free to apply. Set up one profile and enter as many lotteries as you want.

There is never a fee for an HPD or HDC housing application. Anyone charging you is a scam.

Apply to lotteries

NYCHA — public housing and Section 8

Apply for public housing and check where you stand on the waitlist.

HPD eviction prevention

The city's guide to your rights as a tenant, repairs, harassment and rent arrears help.

CityFHEPS — rental assistance voucher

A city voucher that pays most of your rent every month. For people in shelter or at risk of losing their home.

Homebase can help you apply. Start there if you are not in shelter yet.

Money & Bills 10 resources

Cash Assistance

Monthly cash for rent, food and basics if you have little or no income. Same application as SNAP.

Apply now (718) 557-1399

Fair Fares NYC — half-price subway and bus

Half off subway, eligible buses and Access-A-Ride. Open to New Yorkers 16 to 84 at or below 145% of the federal poverty line.

Fair Fares now issues OMNY cards instead of MetroCards.

Apply for Fair Fares

NYC Free Tax Prep

File your taxes free with an IRS-certified preparer and keep the whole refund. Roughly $68,000 or less filing single, $97,000 or less with family.

Book free tax prep Call 311

Financial Empowerment Centers — free money counseling

Sit down one-on-one with a counselor about debt, credit, budgeting or opening a bank account. Free, in every borough, no matter your income.

Book a session Call 311

HEAP — heating and cooling bill help

Pays part of your heating or cooling bill, and can fix or replace a broken furnace or air conditioner. Money goes straight to the utility.

Application windows open and close each season. Call to check what is open now.

Apply for HEAP (718) 557-1399

Con Edison payment help

Set up a payment agreement, stop a shutoff, or apply for bill credits.

Set up a plan

IDNYC — free photo ID

A free government photo ID for anyone 10 and up living in NYC. Immigration status does not matter. Gets you into banks, clinics, schools and museums.

Get an IDNYC

HRA Job Centers and SNAP Centers

Where to go in person for Cash Assistance, SNAP and Medicaid if you cannot do it online. There are centers in every borough.

Find a center (718) 557-1399

Social Security, SSI and disability

Apply for retirement, SSI or disability benefits. You can start the application online and finish by phone.

Apply for benefits 1-800-772-1213

National Grid bill help

Payment plans, budget billing and low-income discounts if your gas bill is behind.

Get bill help

Unemployment 3 resources

File an unemployment claim

File the week you lose your job. Do not wait. You file once, then certify every week to get paid.

Telephone Claims Center, Monday to Friday 8am to 5pm. Press 9 for an interpreter.

File your claim 1-888-209-8124

Certify weekly for your benefit

You have to certify every single week or you do not get paid, even after you are approved.

Certify now

Problem with your claim

Denied, stuck, overpaid, or waiting too long. This is where you push back and where you appeal.

Get claim help 1-888-209-8124

SBA funding programs

Federal loans, microloans, disaster loans and surety bonds. The SBA does not usually lend directly, it guarantees the loan so a bank will say yes.

Real SBA grants are rare and go to specific programs. Anyone who charges you a fee to get an SBA grant is a scam.

See funding
